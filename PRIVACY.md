# Privacy

Hum collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no internet permission, so it cannot.

It keeps, in its own private storage on the phone: each take's recording, the notes found in
it and the pitch the voice took, the take's name and settings, and the app's few settings.
Android does not back it up, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. A take deleted in the
app is deleted with its recording.

The microphone is used only between pressing Record and pressing Stop, and a notification shows
for as long as it is. **Read a recording** reads only the file you pick.

**Send** writes a take as a file and hands it to the app you choose in the share sheet, and only
when you press it. Where it goes after that is up to you.

The About dialog's last line opens a web address in your browser, and only when you press it.
