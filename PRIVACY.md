# Privacy

Hum collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no internet permission, so it cannot.

It keeps, in its own private storage on the phone: each take's recording, the notes found in
it and the pitch the voice took, the take's name and settings, and the app's few settings.
None of it goes into Android's cloud backup or a phone-to-phone transfer, and uninstalling the
app removes all of it. A take deleted in the app is deleted with its recording.

The microphone is used only between pressing Record and pressing Stop, and a notification shows
for as long as it is. **Read a recording** reads only the file you pick.

**Send** writes a take as a file and hands it to the app you choose in the share sheet, and only
when you press it. Where it goes after that is up to you. **Save as** writes a take (MIDI, sheet
music or the recording) to the place you choose in Android's own file picker, and only when you
press it; that place can be a folder on the phone or another app's storage, such as a Nextcloud
folder, and what that app does with the file is up to it.

**Save a backup** (Settings) writes every take, with its recording, notes and pitch, and the
app's settings to one file you choose through the same file picker, and only when you ask.
**Restore from a backup** reads such a file the same way, checks all of it first, and only then
replaces the takes and settings; a wrong file changes nothing. Since Android's own backup and
transfer leave the app out, this file is the way to move the whole library to another phone; it
goes only where you put it, and uninstalling the app does not remove it.

The About dialog also holds the app's privacy and terms in short, and its source and license
links, the site address and the last line (the llama) each open a web address in your browser,
only when you press them.

## Who we are

Hum is made by wander wildwood, an independent developer: one person, not a company. wander wildwood is the data controller for anything the app itself handles, and that is very little. Hum keeps what you put in it on your phone and sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you.

## How to contact us

Write to email@wanderthe.dev about your data, this policy or anything else. The same address is in the app, under About.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to:

- see the personal data held about you (access)
- have it corrected (rectification) or deleted (erasure)
- limit how it is used (restriction)
- object to its use (objection)
- get a copy to take elsewhere (portability)
- withdraw your consent at any time, where something rests on it
- complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live

Similar laws elsewhere, such as the CCPA in California, give you the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Nothing from Hum is sold or shared.

Since Hum keeps what it stores only on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights yourself: delete a take in the app, save a copy of every take with Save a backup, clear the app's data in Android's settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of it. For anything you cannot do yourself, write to the address above.
